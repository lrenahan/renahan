Making
Cyber Security
Make Sense

I'm a cyber security specialist with around 15 years of experience helping organisations understand and defend against the threats that actually keep CISOs up at night. By day I work on the vendor side, protecting some of the world's largest organisations from real-world attacks. Outside of that, I write books, make videos, and try to make cyber security make sense — for everyone. I believe the biggest problem in security isn't the technology. It's communication. Too much of the industry talks to itself, in acronyms, for an audience that already agrees. I'd rather explain a phishing attack using a pub analogy than a MITRE ATT&CK matrix — not because the matrix isn't useful, but because most people will never read it.

If / Else

Six months after burning his old life to the ground, Paul is a digital ghost living by a strict binary code: if an action risks discovery, don't do it. Now hiding in the rural quiet of Orange he is content to remain a shadow — until he encounters a girl and a conspiracy that his logic won't let him ignore. As the physical toll of the "Pause" begins to drain his body and his former pursuers close the distance, Finn must decide if his "if/else" survival strategy is a shield or a cage, and whether he’s willing to crash the system to find the truth.
