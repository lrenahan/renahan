System Pause

Paul, a brilliant cyber security analyst with a dark past as a hacker, is on the verge of proposing to Kat, his pregnant girlfriend. But fate intervenes when he is brutally attacked, seemingly without motive. His life unravels further when his team at Australia Bank uncover a data breach that holds them to ransom.

As Paul dives into the incident investigation, he realizes that Kat has vanished. Desperate to find her, he descends into a murky underworld of secrets, betrayal, and greed. The shocking truth emerges: Kali Kartel, a ruthless hacking syndicate, are hell-bent on infiltrating the bank's systems and will stop at nothing to achieve their goals.

Haunted by his past and racing against time, Paul faces a dual challenge. Should he prioritize solving the cryptic puzzle of the breach or focus on locating Kat? Each decision leads him deeper into a web of deception, where shadows conceal deadly secrets and danger lurks around every corner.

Grounded in the genuine threat landscape, system("PAUSE"); is a gripping techno-thriller that blends cyber intrigue, a whodunit, an origin tale, and a love story that may not be what it seems. With its thought-provoking exploration of ethical use of abilities and real-world cybercrime methods, strategies, and processes, this book will keep readers on the edge of their seats until the very end.

If / Else

Six months after burning his old life to the ground, Paul is a digital ghost living by a strict binary code: if an action risks discovery, don't do it. Now hiding in the rural quiet of Orange he is content to remain a shadow — until he encounters a girl and a conspiracy that his logic won't let him ignore. As the physical toll of the "Pause" begins to drain his body and his former pursuers close the distance, Finn must decide if his "if/else" survival strategy is a shield or a cage, and whether he’s willing to crash the system to find the truth.
